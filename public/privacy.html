Privacy Policy

Smart Leave Permission System · Last updated: 19 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Smart Leave Permission System ("the System") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The System is used by students, faculty and administrators of the college to apply for, review and track leave.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, role (student, faculty or admin), department, and an optional phone number.
  • Sign-in information: if you use "Continue with Google", we receive your name and verified email address from Google. We do not receive or store your Google password. If you create an email account, your password is stored only as a secure one-way hash.
  • Leave information: leave type, dates, reason, status, reviewer remarks, and any PDF or document you choose to upload as supporting proof.
  • Notifications: messages generated by the System about your requests.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To submit leave requests and let faculty and administrators review them.
  • To send in-app notifications about request status.
  • To produce dashboards, analytics and reports for the college.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who can see your information

  • Students can see only their own requests and documents.
  • Faculty can see leave requests, reasons and uploaded documents of students in their own department.
  • Administrators can see all users, requests, documents and reports.

Medical and personal documents are shared only with people who need them to process your request.

4. Google user data

When you sign in with Google, the System uses only your basic profile (name and email) to identify you and create your account. This data is not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not transferred except as needed to operate the System.

5. Storage and security

Data is stored on the server where the System is installed. Passwords are hashed, sign-in sessions use signed tokens that expire after 7 days, and uploaded documents are only available to authorised users. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your login details private and sign out on shared computers.

6. Data retention and deletion

Leave records and documents are kept for as long as the college needs them for academic and administrative purposes. You can ask the System administrator to correct or delete your account and data, subject to the college's record-keeping rules.

7. Cookies and local storage

The System stores your sign-in token and display preferences (such as dark mode) in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Children

The System is intended for college students and staff. It is not directed at young children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.

10. Contact
